# Hufu 虎符: Compliance mapping

**What Hufu is, legally.** Wallet software that runs on the student's own device and controls a vault the student owns. It does not issue stablecoins, hold customer money, exchange currencies for others, or onboard customers. So most rules below apply to the *institutions around it* (stablecoin issuers, banks, virtual-asset service providers). This document shows, rule by rule, which part falls to wallet software and which Hufu control implements it. In the prototype it runs on test tokens only.

Sources are marked **[primary]** (the regulator or the law itself) or **[secondary]** (law-firm or news summaries, used where we could not retrieve the official text). This is an engineering mapping, not legal advice.

## 1. Rule-by-rule mapping

| # | Rule | What it asks, as relevant here | Hufu control | Code | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hong Kong Stablecoins Ordinance (Cap. 656)**, in force 1 Aug 2025 [S1][S2] | Only HKMA-licensed issuers may issue fiat-referenced stablecoins in Hong Kong or offer them to retail users | Hufu issues nothing. Which tokens the vault accepts is the student's on-chain policy (`setTokenPolicy`, owner-only); production would enable only licensed issuers' coins. The demo uses test tokens | `HufuVault.sol` | Design constraint, enforced on-chain |
| 2 | **HKMA AML/CFT Guideline for licensed stablecoin issuers** (Aug 2025) [S3] | Issuers screen wallets (blockchain analytics), treat unhosted wallets with care, and may blacklist illicit addresses | **Counterparty screening:** every recipient is checked before Hufu pays it, files a request for it or saves it; checked again when the student approves; saved payees re-screened by the autopilot | `compliance.py` · `guard/core.py` · `approvals.py` · `autopilot.py` | Implemented |
| 3 | **Sanctions: OFAC SDN list**, which includes digital-currency addresses [S4] | No dealings with listed persons; listed addresses are published with the entries | Same screening. `make sanctions-update` loads every 0x address on the SDN list. A hit is a **BLOCK even when the student asks**: sanctions are not a matter of consent | `sanctions_update.py` · `data/compliance/ofac_eth.txt` | Implemented: 122 OFAC SDN addresses (fetched 19 Oct 2026) + a demo entry |
| 4 | **Travel rule:** FATF Recommendation 16 [S5]; Hong Kong AMLO (Cap. 615) and SFC guideline for virtual-asset transfers, with a fuller data set at **HK$8,000** or more [S6][S7] | Institutions sending or receiving virtual assets exchange originator and beneficiary information. A self-hosted wallet's counterparty institution may ask for it | **Transfer records:** every transfer of HK$8,000 or more (tUSD converted at the pool rate) gets a record with originator, beneficiary, amount, purpose and transaction. Kept on the device, never on-chain; shown in the dashboard's Compliance tab | `compliance.record_transfer` · `/compliance` | Implemented |
| 5 | **Structuring** (splitting a payment to stay under a threshold), a standard AML red flag (FATF, *Virtual Assets Red Flag Indicators*, 2020) | Detect and stop split payments | **Per-payee daily cap on-chain** (`paidToPayeeOnDay`). The guard asks when the payee's daily allowance is used up. Found by our red team (A23) and fixed | `HufuVault.sol` · `guard/core.py` | Implemented |
| 6 | **NFRA, Guiding Opinions on the Safe Development and Application of AI in Banking and Insurance** (June 2026) [S8][S9] | Applications involving financial transactions are high-risk. They need human oversight and intervention at critical stages, defined emergency deactivation, and a manual fallback. Customers' personal information must not be used to train generative models | **Human approval (T2)** for anything beyond the limits the student signed. **Freeze** at any time by any role, with no approval needed (emergency deactivation). The **approval queue** is the manual fallback, and the vault's limits hold even if the AI is off. The **local model** is never trained on user data | `HufuVault.sol` · `guard/` · `approvals.py` | Implemented. These rules bind banks and insurers such as WeBank: Hufu is built so an institution could deploy it under them |
| 7 | **HKMA circular on consumer protection in the use of generative AI** (19 Aug 2024) [S10] | Human-in-the-loop in early deployment; a clearly defined scope; a way to ask for review; using AI to send fraud alerts is encouraged | **Defined scope:** 16 tools, and no tool can change a rule (T3). Human approval; a plain-language reason for every decision; the autopilot's scam-email and stolen-key alerts | `tools.py` · `autopilot.py` | Implemented |
| 8 | **TC260 security guidelines for deploying and using AI agents** (July 2026) [S11]; draft national standard on agent application security [S12] | Strict permission control over the agent's lifecycle. The draft standard lists human intervention for high-risk operations, logging and emergency shutdown | Permission tiers enforced in code and on-chain; hash-chained audit log anchored on-chain; freeze | `guard/audit.py` · `HufuVault.sol` | Implemented |
| 9 | **Bangladesh Bank notices** on virtual currencies (2014, 2017, 2021, 2022) [S13][S14] | Virtual currencies are not recognised under the Foreign Exchange Regulation Act 1947; dealing in them risks breaching the Money Laundering Prevention Act 2012 and the Anti-Terrorism Act 2009 | **Huitong** never offers stablecoin or crypto routes to or from Bangladesh, only bank and licensed money-service routes, and shows the reason. The agent's instructions forbid suggesting them | `huitong.py` · `remittance.yaml` | Implemented |
| 10 | **PBoC and nine other authorities: Notice on further preventing and disposing of the risks of virtual-currency trading speculation** (Sept 2021, Yinfa [2021] No. 237) [S15] | Virtual currencies are not legal tender. Exchange, matching and related services are illegal financial activities for mainland residents | No crypto route for mainland China corridors. Hufu makes payments only: no exchange or trading service. The persona and the stablecoin use are in Hong Kong | `huitong.py` | Implemented as a jurisdiction rule |
| 11 | **Hong Kong Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486)** [S16] | Data protection principles: purpose limitation, data minimisation, security | Local model, so no chat text leaves the device. Memo text stays local and only its hash goes on-chain; the audit log is on the device with only its head on-chain | `store.py` · `guard/audit.py` | Prototype |
| 12 | **KYC / customer due diligence:** for stablecoin issuers, at issuance and redemption, including at HK$8,000 or more [S3] | Identify and verify the customer | Falls on the licensed issuer or on-ramp. Hufu onboards no one and holds no fiat | n/a | Out of scope (stated) |

## 2. What Hufu does not cover

- **KYC and suspicious-transaction reporting** are the job of the licensed institutions. Hufu can export its transfer records to support them.
- **Screening depth.** The prototype checks exact addresses: the project's list plus the OFAC SDN list. It does not screen people who aren't on the list (politically exposed persons), and it has no commercial blockchain analytics, so it can't tell whether an address is a step or two away from a listed one.
- **Travel-rule messaging.** Records are kept for the student to share. There is no institution-to-institution protocol such as TRISA or TRP.
- **Legal review.** A production launch in Hong Kong or Bangladesh would need counsel and a licensing analysis.
